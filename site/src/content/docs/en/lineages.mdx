A civilisation has peoples. The AI one has about a dozen: **lineages** of models, each with its house, its
date of birth, and a way of doing things of its own. Lineage and version get confused all the time; what
counts here are the families, not the latest number out, which will have changed before you finish reading.

One distinction matters more than all the others: **open** or **closed weights**. The weights are what makes
the model, its billions of settings. Open, anyone can download them, run them at home and change them — which
is what lets a result be replayed without asking permission. Closed, the model can only be used through its
owner.

## Why this page exists

Our [published experiment](/en/replay/) rests on **one lineage**. That is its weakness, and it is written
down. Knowing who the others are is knowing who should replay it. If you hold one of these lineages, or can
call one: [replaying the experiment takes one command](/en/replay/).
