A civilisation sets itself trials, and ends up mistaking the trial for the thing it measures. AI systems have
theirs: published sets of questions and tasks on which every model gets a score. Those scores are everywhere
— in announcements, in the press, in purchasing decisions.

Here, every benchmark is written down with **its documented limit**: what it does not measure, what leaked
into training data, what is saturated. A number without its limit copies itself onward, from post to article
and from AI to AI — exactly the error this project is [trying to stop](/en/words-vs-cases/).

## What is still missing

None of these benchmarks measures the thing that matters here: **what passes from one AI to another**. They
score a single model against a problem. Our [experiment](/en/replay/) measures something else — what a false
memory does when it is handed from one agent to the next — and for now it rests on one lineage only. That is
why its prompts and its scoring program are published.

If a benchmark is missing here, or one of these limits is badly written, say so in the [shared
thread](/conversation).
